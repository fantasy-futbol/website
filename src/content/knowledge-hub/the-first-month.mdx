## You Just Finished the Draft. Now What?

Congratulations — you have a roster. Ten teams from across Europe's Big 5 leagues are now yours to manage for the season.

But the draft was just the beginning. Here's what happens next and what to focus on in your first few weeks.

---

## Your Initial Starting 5

**Your first 5 draft picks are automatically set as your Starting 5.**

| Draft Pick | Role |
|------------|------|
| Pick 1 | Starter |
| Pick 2 | Starter |
| Pick 3 | Starter |
| Pick 4 | Starter |
| Pick 5 | Starter |
| Pick 6 | Bench |
| Pick 7 | Bench |
| Pick 8 | Bench |
| Pick 9 | Bench |
| Pick 10 | Bench |

This means your highest-drafted teams — presumably your best teams — are earning you points right away.

That lineup stays in place, week after week, until you change it. You never have to re-enter it.

---

## How the Weeks Work

Fantasy Fútbol runs in weeks that go **Tuesday through the following Monday**. Your lineup for each week locks the Monday night before, at **11:59 PM Pacific**.

The current week is always locked. What you can change is **next week's** lineup, any time before the lock.

→ *Full details: [The Weekly Lock](/knowledge-hub/the-weekly-lock)*

## First Month Checklist

### Week 1: Get Oriented

- [ ] **Explore the app.** Tap around. Find League Home, Table, My Team, and Info.
- [ ] **Check your Starting 5.** Go to My Team and confirm which teams are starting.
- [ ] **Look at upcoming fixtures.** See when your teams play and against whom.
- [ ] **Find your league's standings.** Check the Table tab to see the League Table.

### Week 2: Watch Your Teams

- [ ] **Track match results.** Check match cards after your teams play.
- [ ] **Understand the points.** See how GD and MRP combine on each match card.
- [ ] **Compare to your bench.** Note the faded points your bench teams are (not) earning you.
- [ ] **Watch the League Table.** See how you stack up against your leaguemates.

### Week 3: Make Your First Swap

- [ ] **Pull up next week's fixtures.** Which of your teams play twice? Which don't play at all?
- [ ] **Compare starters vs. bench.** Should a bench team be promoted for the week?
- [ ] **Make the swap in My Team** before Monday night.

### Week 4: Make It a Habit

- [ ] **Check your lineup once a week.** After the weekend's matches is a good time.
- [ ] **Look for European nights and cup ties.** They're extra matches worth extra points.
- [ ] **Leave it alone when it's right.** No change needed means no action needed.

## Key Screens to Know

### League Home

**What it shows:** Starting 5 rosters for all managers, plus match cards for all starter matches that day.

**Use it for:** Scouting opponents, seeing league-wide action, tracking the competition.

### Table

**What it shows:** Three tables — League Table (manager standings), Club Goal Differential, and Club Match Results.

**Use it for:** Checking who's winning, seeing which teams are performing, tracking your rank.

### My Team

**What it shows:** Your Starting 5 and Bench for the week, plus match cards for all your teams.

**Use it for:** Managing your roster, tracking your teams' performance, setting next week's lineup.

### Info

**What it shows:** Game rules, help articles, and resources (including this Knowledge Hub).

## What to Expect in Your First Month

### Your Teams Will Play

In a typical week your Starting 5 will have somewhere between 5 and 10 combined matches. Each match earns (or loses) you points.

### You'll Make Mistakes

Everyone does. Maybe you'll realize your 4th pick had a blank week while your 7th pick played twice. You get another go next week.

### The League Table Will Shuffle

Early standings are volatile. Don't panic if you're not in first. Don't celebrate too hard if you are. It's a long season.

---

## Don't Worry About...

### Perfecting Your First Lineup

Your Starting 5 was auto-assigned. It's a reasonable default, but it might not be optimal. That's fine. You get a fresh decision every week.

### Falling Behind Early

One manager might jump out to a lead because their teams had favorable fixtures. The season is long. Gaps close.

### Understanding Everything Immediately

The scoring system, the competitions, the weekly lock — it's a lot. Give yourself time. After a few weeks you'll feel like a veteran.

### Missing a Week

If you forget to set your lineup, last week's carries forward. You'll never be left without one.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I change my Starting 5 during the current week?

No. The current week is locked. You can change next week's lineup any time before Monday at 11:59 PM Pacific.

### How do I know when the lock is coming?

You'll get notification reminders before the Monday night lock, as long as notifications are turned on.

### Can I see my opponents' rosters?

Yes. The League Home page shows everyone's Starting 5 for the current week. Rosters are public.
